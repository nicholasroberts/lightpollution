# Country archetypes: demography, lighting operation and spectral substitution

**Date:** 21 October 2026. **Status:** documented, hypothesis-led country classification for manuscript; **NOT** a causal attribution of a fraction of VIIRS or PRC change to a mechanism.

## Data definitions and limitations

The observed demographic comparison is from **2014 to 2020** on **fixed NUTS2016 NUTS3** geography (1,317 complete matched regions overall). For each region the demographic predictor is **2014→2020 absolute change in population density, in people/km²**, and each biological response is **absolute change in spider or moth polarization-receptor contrast (ΔPRC)** inferred from the independently locked spider17 and moth16 VIIRS-band response functions. Both use the approved snow-corrected PRC series (2021 correction irrelevant to this 2014–20 interval). The main descriptive sign thresholds are |Δdensity| >5 people/km² and |ΔPRC| >0.001; correlations use **all** continuous observed values, not only thresholded cases.

The country rank associations (Spearman) come directly from user-uploaded \`country_directional_summary_2014_2020(1).csv\` and \`absolute_vs_percentage_correlation_sensitivity.csv\`. Correlations are **within NUTS3 regions in a country**, not comparisons of national mean changes. The two taxa are not independent physical satellite replicates; both transform the same annual VIIRS field. Nominal correlation significance must be interpreted cautiously given spatial autocorrelation, national economic confounders, and especially the suspected **2019→2020 Eurostat data discontinuity/very low variation**. Re-evaluate 2014→2019 with fixed cohort before paper-level statistical claims.

### How the groups differ scientifically

- **Demographic-growth-compatible deterioration** is particularly compelling in TR (ρ≈−0.57), where population-linked settlement/infrastructure development is a defensible *hypothesis* rather than a demonstrated causal pathway. **DE is not assigned this same mechanism** despite ρ≈−0.36 and population-density increases: its settlement stage and demographic context differ markedly from Türkiye, and existing German lighting stock, municipal switching policy and LED renovation may affect night radiance independently of new infrastructure. Nominal within-country significance needs population-source QA and spatially aware uncertainty.
- **Recovery without depopulation** is best demonstrated by FR, a strong policy *candidate* with independently documented restrictions and subsequent broad midnight switching. **Temporal alignment crucial:** the widely cited **2022 French streetlighting energy crisis and Enedis May2022→May2023 −34.2% at 2am cannot explain our 2014→2020 population–PRC observations**. It is relevant to the separate full **2014→2024** radiance narrative only. To attribute FR 2014→2020 PRC recovery to lighting policies, match individual municipal curfew start dates before 2021. The 2013 regulation covers nonresidential lighting, NOT mandatory all-streetlight switches.
- **Deterioration without population growth** (IT, RO) is a direct counterexample to an exclusive population-infrastructure driver. Italian LED conversion is *independent evidence that radiance can be spectrally mis-measured*, not proof that LED switches caused Italian PRC loss; in fact, transition to bluer LEDs can make measured DNB **understate** some changes in biologically relevant blue emission. ISS colour ratios, instrument spectral response and physiological wavelength sensitivity are needed.
- **Unresolved/no single link** (NL, PL, Greece, and mechanism-wise DE) shows not to force each country into a narrative. The Netherlands particularly needs NL333 source QA due to extreme greenhouse/outlier values.

## Ireland (Category 2) and Germany (separate mechanism class): current evidence boundary

- **Ireland: include in Category 2 for comparison, NOT yet as a confirmed positive-PRC correlation.** Independent Sustainable Energy Authority of Ireland (SEAI) project accounts date LED streetlight conversion to 2016–2020 in Monaghan and Cavan, so there is a genuinely time-aligned source-change mechanism (https://www.seai.ie/case-studies/monaghan-lighting-upgrade ; https://www.seai.ie/case-studies/cavan-county-council). The 2022 ISS European spectral study includes Dublin comparisons, but its 2012–13 vs 2014–20 epoch contrast is NOT an annual source-conversion calendar. The national Public Lighting Energy Efficiency Project published in 2024 (https://www.localgov.ie/national-programmes/national-public-lighting-energy-efficiency-project) must NOT be backdated as evidence that all Ireland was converted or dimmed in 2014–20. Before giving Ireland a quantitative PRC sign/ρ, extract its **IE rows and matched NUTS3 count from the original EcoVis country summary**, ideally comparing 2014–2019 and 2014–2020.
- **Germany: distinguish association from explanation.** Official 2018 Geilenkirchen council proposal 1385/2018 (https://stadt-gk.de/bi/vo0050.asp?__kvonr=3380) records a 2012 policy switching off alternate streetlights from 21:00–06:00, approximately 45% of fixtures converted to LED by 2018, and a *proposal* to return all lamps to overnight operation with possible dimming. This establishes that municipal lighting operation can change in either direction, NOT that the proposed change was implemented nationwide or that it caused the observed DE ρ. Hessian municipalities show different local night-off and LED regimes (https://www.lichtverschmutzung-hessen.de/Nachtabschaltung), but the map is compiled predominantly from 2022–23 reports and cannot independently establish timing for 2014–20. German federal energy-saving lighting restrictions from late 2022 do NOT explain 2014–20. Specific dated municipality records and luminaire inventories remain needed before asserting that comparatively limited switch-offs, new infrastructure or LED transformations drove the DE trend.
- **Category labels are working mechanistic hypotheses, not fitted classifications.** The underlying NUTS3 country correlations are descriptive; LED change can decrease VIIRS DNB radiance without proportionate decrease in ecological blue-light exposure. Do not equate LED energy savings, satellite brightness and biological PRC gain.

## Suggested manuscript comparison

**Four initial archetype panels, each with year-matched NUTS3 Δdensity (people/km²) vs absolute spider ΔPRC and moth inset/colour markers, at 2014→2019 and 2014→2020**: (A) Türkiye expansion-associated deterioration; (B) Germany negative rank association with *heterogeneous/unknown* operational-lighting context, NOT the same development mechanism as Türkiye; (C) France recovery despite population growth; (D) Italy losses despite population decline. **Ireland belongs in Category 2's lighting-transition comparators**, but plot/label its quantitative demographic–PRC direction only after checking its rows and 2014–2019 coverage. Belgian Walloon municipalities are another documented operational-lighting comparison. Romania, Netherlands and Portugal are supporting/sensitivity examples, not primary forced explanatory labels.

Each panel should have at least country sample size, Spearman ρ (with spatial uncertainty caution), median density changes, proportion of +ΔD/−ΔPRC and +ΔD/+ΔPRC regions, and matched policy/spectral timeline. Make a country-specific **2014–2019 sensitivity** before using 2020 because most countries' 2019→20 population changes were nearly constant or unidentifiable in earlier analyses. Limit causal language to "consistent with", "despite", or "associated with" until independent municipality/energy/LED field data are spatially matched. Also avoid mixing 2014–20 population evidence directly with endpoint 2014–24 radiance/PRC results in the same numerical claim.

**Overall thesis:** the population-density–radiance cross-sectional coupling does not uniquely determine **temporal** ecological-contrast change: demographic growth *can* accompany deterioration, whereas recovery may occur independently through active management and spectral substitution may make the satellite record an incomplete proxy for biologically relevant light.
