# LED-dominated historical contrast cases: independent source audit (10 Nov 2026)

**Purpose:** Find two large, documented sodium→LED transitions that can be compared to Coventry/Échirolles *genuine operating-hours switch-offs*. Crucially, **LED installation is not equivalent to no dimming**, and **electricity savings are not equivalent to constant emitted lumens**. No VIIRS trend has been examined or used to pick cases. The two preferred candidates below are **not certified LED-only controls**, pending independent verification of night-time dimming profiles.

## Shortlist and factual evidence

|---|---|---|---|---|

## Rejected as pure 'LED without dimming' cases: original documentary counter-evidence

- **Plymouth, England: REJECT strict LED-only.** The *original 29 April 2014 Plymouth City Council contract*, sections **4.1–4.6**, states **automatic dimming equipment was included for main roads** in the ~28,857-lantern rollout and that replacement photocells would shorten operational hours; residential lights were not automatically dimmed in the original proposal. The council's **February 2023 new smart management/dimming** cannot retroactively establish 2014–2017 as non-dimmed. Later work is independent additional control, not the first ever dimming. [Original contract PDF](https://democracy.plymouth.gov.uk/documents/s54500/Award%20of%20contract%20for%20the%20provision%20of%20low%20energy%20street%20lighting%20luminaires.pdf); [2023 council story](https://www.plymouth.gov.uk/news/plymouth-get-smart-street-lighting).
- **Milan, Italy: REJECT pure LED-only.** ~140,000 changed June 2014–Dec 2015 (the August completion target mostly streetlights). The supplier **Osram confirms AstroDim automatic night-time dimming** as part of this original installation. [City of Milan account](https://pgt.comune.milano.it/vasraall2-quadro-di-riferimento-territoriale-e-ambientale/2-analisi-dei-fattori-determinanti/26-sistema-dei-sottoservizi/264-rete-teleriscaldamento-rete-illuminazione-pubblica-e-impianti-semaforici); [Osram announcement](https://ams-osram.com/news/press-releases/pr-28-04-2015a).
- **Turin, Italy: REJECT pure LED-only.** ~55,000 changed in 2015 but technical follow-up explicitly says **stepped overnight dimming control** was integrated. [Measured follow-up](https://www.mdpi.com/2071-1050/15/24/16910).
- **Richmond caveat**: *unchanged operating HOURS is NOT unchanged output at 01:30*; its council documents setting a dimming profile after installation. It is not a certified undimmed case.

## Methods and next qualification gate

Independent validation needed before comparing candidate VIIRS:
1. Obtain per-street/cabinet time-appropriate **actual night-time dimming profiles, not just 'capability to dim'**. For Rome, also distinguish standard streetlights vs archaeological/artistic lighting and actual installed lumen flux by year. For Richmond, determine the **2019–2022 01:30** dimming set points and the roll-out completion date.
2. Compare **municipality-only monthly** VNL V1 radiation at *identical pixel supports* with same-month baselines, \`cf_cvg>=1,3,5\`, pre-trend and radiance map hotspot checks. Do not choose cities by VIIRS direction of change.
3. Compare separately to **locked NUTS2016 annual** VNL V2 regional radiance and the frozen spider/moth PRC endpoint results. For Richmond UKI75, Hounslow is a co-member; for Rome metropolitan ITI43, the municipality is only part of the region. Never directly subtract monthly municipal VNL1 values from annual regional VNL2.
4. Never equate streetlight electrical energy savings to reduced lumens. **Rome gives a powerful positive demonstration:** 2017→2018 **luminous flux ~flat while energy fell 27.4%** during large LED conversion. However its whole 2014–18 flux genuinely declined, so this one-year result does not generalise to all years. It may be more effective to plot actual installed luminous flux and VIIRS side by side, rather than to assert Rome is a no-dimming case.
5. If no two cases have documented *unchanged at-overpass output and unchanged operating hours*, retain them as **LED-dominated contrasts**, not definitive LED-only controls. The negative evidence audit is a scientific deliverable, not an inconvenience.

**No LED response direction, large/small NUTS3 decline, or formal effect estimate has been assumed or computed.** Both cases are prospective. The two frozen NUTS3 dimming cases, Coventry/Échirolles, remain the primary operation-change evidence.
