## Permission, provenance and reproducibility

- Source titles, formats and dates are recorded for later retrieval. For any original document copied into the repository, check publisher licensing and attribution first; avoid republishing third-party PDFs/XLSX without rights.
- Public current asset layers (Kent and Bristol) are **current snapshots**: no inference about asset status at historical intervention dates without historic records.
- The Coventry street-list PDF/XLSX is an authoritative *list of street names and ward codes*, not an audited per-lamp polygon; exact original list revision/date needs recording.
- The Échirolles metropolitan PDF contains meter evidence for an October **2022** change; the presentation's action table appears inconsistent (labels '2023'); contemporary 2022 municipal notices are preferred for implementation date.
- These sources support Stage A design, not the existence/magnitude/significance of any local DNB response.
