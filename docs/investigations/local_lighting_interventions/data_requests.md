# Data required to finish Stage A — authorities and infrastructure owners

**Prepared 9 November 2026.** Proposed requests, not messages sent. Avoid personal data and ask for anonymised operational streetlighting asset history. All requested data are for scientific environmental satellite validation.

## Common fields for all councils

Unique pole/lantern and feeder IDs; point geometry (British National Grid / WGS84 or local projection), operator and ownership; original/replacement lamp types and power/lumens/SPD/CCT; actual dates of installation, control-node commissioning, switch-off or dimming; actual operating profiles for each day around VIIRS early-morning overpass; exception map; pre/post electricity consumption split by feeder and lamp if available; historic 2014–24 snapshots, uncertainty and revision dates. Ask for persistent asset identifiers, not postal addresses of residents.

## Requests specific to cases

1. **Échirolles:** 2022 streetlight circuits and excluded axes, early rue/square Eugène-Sue and Pathé parking exact extents, minute-level change on 1 October 2022, `EP CITE poste 079` monthly consumption and 2022–24 LED installation log. Query Échirolles mairie / Grenoble-Alpes Métropole responsible lighting department; source presentation is [here](https://planclimat.grenoblealpesmetropole.fr/cms_viewFile.php?idtf=1784&path=Parole-aux-partenaires.pdf).
2. **Coventry:** request XLSX/PDF version and exact column list behind council's [affected street/ward list](https://www.coventry.gov.uk/downloads/download/7837/part-night-switch-off-alphabetical-list-of-streets-and-ward), including May–July 2024 per-lamp commissioning and permanent exemption list, lamp pre-2025 technology and energy usage. 8 Oct 2024 FOI [source](https://www.coventry.gov.uk/downloads/file/43696/foi648678140-redacted) provides fleet counts.
3. **Portsmouth:** original 2019 adaptive-lighting controlled trial protocol; identify which of Baffins/Cosham/St Jude/Milton/Fratton was the unchanged 'placebo' site, precise trial polygons and 2020 CMS changes; technology and prior spectra per column. Council's [guidance](https://www.portsmouth.gov.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Guidance-for-dealing-with-stray-and-unwanted-light-from-Street-lights-0622.pdf) verifies citywide summer 2020 change.
4. **Thanet:** Kent County Council street-lighting asset team: 2015–19 historical stock and asset IDs, conversion phases, actual all-night switch-on date, previous 2014–16 part-night schedules, concrete-column exemptions. Current [location CSV](https://www.kent.gov.uk/roads-and-travel/what-we-look-after/street-lights/faulty-street-lights) and [GIS layer](https://webapps.kent.gov.uk/server/rest/services/Test/KentViewPortalTest/MapServer/44) may link by asset ID if old records available. Kent FOI route **freedomofinformation@kent.gov.uk** is referenced by [Dover Council](https://www.dover.gov.uk/Corporate-Information/Freedom-of-Information/Freedom-of-information-Disclosure-Log/Responses/FI15314.aspx).
5. **Bristol:** ask **lighting@bristol.gov.uk** for 2023–24 historical asset-ID/date installation snapshots from council and Centregreat, CMS-only vs lantern swaps, historical 38 schedule sets and 30-minute power/status data, technical specification/CCT and old technology. Current [GIS source](https://opendata.bristol.gov.uk/datasets/bcc::streetlights-3) and [project description](https://www.bristol.gov.uk/residents/streets-travel/transport-plans-and-projects/upgrading-to-led-street-lighting) are starting points, not historical classifications.
