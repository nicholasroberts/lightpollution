# Paper 1 analysis plan

## Question

How do fixed solar/lunar geometry and artificial-light spectra determine the
polarization cue available to an animal?

All sites are in scope. WClawn and Oxford Business Park are pilot results, not
the final study population. Pending sites remain visible in the ledger.

## Five figures

| Figure | Question | Analysis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | When does the natural pattern require both Sun and Moon? | Experimental/model DoLP and spatial AoP structure across twilight and lunar-dominated captures; independent Moon/orientation evidence. |
| 2 | How does added light change the usable pattern? | Reproduce site patterns with PRC-fitted dilution; compare spatial patterns, band shape and receptor responses across brightness levels. |
| 3 | How much HPS versus LED produces the same PRC? | Matched-PRC radiant-energy amounts, curves, percentages and spectral/atmospheric sensitivity scenarios. |
| 4 | What happens at equal human luminance? | Normalize sky spectra using photopic V(lambda), then calculate animal PRC. |
| 5 | What does a documented conversion imply? | One verified HPS-to-LED replacement without dimming; use actual fixture and operating evidence where available. |

## Order

1. Audit all sites and independently resolve registration, Moon and analyzer conventions.
2. Establish the natural-pattern comparison and AoP/DoLP assessment criteria.
3. Run PRC-equivalent HPS/LED scenarios across eligible sites on identical support.
4. Evaluate alternative LED spectra, RH1/camera response and atmospheric assumptions.
5. Implement equal photopic sky luminance with an explicitly sourced V(lambda).
6. Select and document a conversion-only example; quantify conditional PRC effects.
7. Finalize the five figures and retain full-site/QC outputs as companions.

## Scientific limits at setup

Current solar/lunar mixture ratios were fitted to DoLP and checked with AoP
changes; no joint AoP objective has been implemented. Current lamp amounts are
fitted to PRC. These are different fitting steps and must remain distinguishable.
Green-camera PRC is the investigator-accepted RH1 approximation. The D850
response check does not uniquely reconstruct animal spectral Stokes.
The natural source spectra are currently shared between Sun and Moon; twilight
colour and atmosphere remain sensitivity tasks.

The pilot 39.1% equivalence is conditional on selected spectra and the receptor,
not a site-independent empirical discovery replicated at two locations. Test
robustness and literature novelty before generalizing. Equal PRC does not force
local DoLP/AoP agreement or establish usable navigation at a behavioral threshold.
