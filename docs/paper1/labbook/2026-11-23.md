## Experimental Stokes audit before Figure 1A

Paused model/figure assembly at investigator request to audit RAW preparation,
saved registration and Stokes. Code review confirms green extraction averages
the two individually black-subtracted Bayer green samples, without gamma,
white balance or per-frame brightness normalization. The reconstruction averages
replicate I,Q,U before computing polarization.

The legacy linear intensity uses I=J0+J90, Q=J0-J90 and signed U=J45-J135.
For ideal equal-throughput nominal analyzers, the equal-weight four-angle least
squares estimate instead uses I=(J0+J45+J90+J135)/2, with the same Q and U.
These agree when pair sums close. Earlier closure discrepancies mean their
difference must be reported, not silently treated as corrected data.

Added audit_spider_measured_stokes.py: verify original NEF hashes/metadata through
the matching preparation manifest when --raw-manifest-root is supplied; re-warp
prepared green frames using saved transforms; independently derive nominal U
sign from landmark roll; compare reconstructed Stokes to saved products;
export both intensity-estimator alternatives, unclipped numeric arrays,
physical-mask TIFFs, camera-frame PNG and audit JSON. No gain/offset fit,
registration refit, sky model, Moon rotation or PRC calculation.

Synthetic tests: both signed analyzer sequences recover known Stokes;
four-angle formula matches independent numpy least squares even for
inconsistent measurements; saved-warp reconstruction and TIFF/PNG export pass.
The original NEF/metadata verification requires EcoVis inputs and is not claimed
tested against real site data here. Fresh real outputs remain pending EcoVis run.
