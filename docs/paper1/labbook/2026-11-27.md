    residual_j(a) = hypot(Q_natural,j, U_natural,j) /

Definitions and interpretation:

- Statistics use 2,825 common spatial bins within each full 60-degree field.
  Spatial SD measures variation across the field, not uncertainty in the mean.
  These are not replacements for the original-resolution Figure 1 statistics.
- Natural-model values describe the locked solar/lunar camera-green pattern
  before adding ALAN. They remain unchanged by the spectral conversion.
- Amounts are added 400–700 nm sky band radiance relative to each site's
  modelled total natural sky band radiance (uniform total natural I=1).
  They are not absolute radiances, electrical power, lumens or inferred site lamp mixtures.
- HPS and LED are separate alternative scenarios, not two simultaneous fitted
  contributions. The museum requires approximately 2.46 times the relative addition
  required at Cottage in either scenario. This does not establish the ratio of
  absolute artificial radiance between sites because the natural references differ.
- The museum DoLP distribution is closely reproduced. Cottage retains more spatial
  variation than the uniform unpolarized-addition model reproduces.
- The Jones update changes inferred band-energy amounts while preserving the
  approved camera-green spatial patterns and their fit residuals. The change is
  not evidence of an improved spatial fit.
- Do not quote the repeated LED/HPS percentage as the Figure 2 result or as
