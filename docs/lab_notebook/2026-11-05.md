## Audit of Figure 1C versus Figure 1D radiance percentage change

Investigator noted Figure 1C visually rises from approximately 1.065 to 1.080 nW cm⁻² sr⁻¹ between 2014 and 2024 and asked why the histogram suggests an approximately +5% change; initial calculation was mis-stated as 13%. Correct arithmetic: (1.080 − 1.065)/1.065 = **1.408%**, not 13%. Figure 1D, not Figure 2D, is the histogram (`src/plot_publication_figure2_population_radiance.py::plot_2d` instead plots country-specific population–radiance scatter).

Code audit: `src/plot_publication_figure1_radiance.py::load_annual` builds Figure 1C from `data/processed/fixed_nuts2016_viirs_2014_2024.parquet` with `radiance_mean_corrected`, and a locked 2021 snow patch substituted at native pixels and aggregated to NUTS3. It calculates annual **European mean** by weighting each region's NUTS3 radiance by the same `valid_area_km2_2014` from the frozen 2014/2024 endpoints, then dividing by sum of areas. The previously uploaded Fig1C PDF explicitly displays **1,522 complete NUTS2016 regions**. The 2021-only snow adjustment cannot change 2014/2024 endpoint percentage.

Figure 1D `endpoint_summary` and `publication_hist` take the independent 1,522-region endpoint CSV `nuts3_radiance_spider_moth_cumulative_change_2014_2024.csv`, compute **regional percentages** `100*(R2024−R2014)/R2014` for every region with positive 2014 radiance, and calculate **equal-NUTS3 arithmetic mean and median**. Histogram uses a signed-log x scale and bins equal in transformed rather than untransformed percentage; its **mode is not the mean/median**. Crucially the percentage change of a weighted European mean corresponds to the regional percentages with **weights proportional to 2014 mapped area × 2014 starting radiance**, *plus the contribution of any initially exact-zero regions bright in 2024*. It need not equal the equal-region histogram centre; neither is an error in itself. Two figure endpoints originate from independent processed tables and are only checked in production with p99 absolute discrepancy tolerance 0.025; their exact agreement requires inspection of local exports.

Added read-only reproducible `src/audit_fig1c_fig1d_radiance_change.py` on `publication-figures`, which reads the existing Figure 1C annual support CSV, the frozen endpoint CSV, and `fig1_provenance_and_summary.json` and prints the **exact** 2014 and 2024 weighted values, Figure 1C percentage, Figure 1D equal-region mean and median, count of zero baselines, recomputed endpoint-area-weighted Europe percentage, weighted-regional-percentage algebraic identity, and any numeric difference between the figure 1C annual parquet-derived and endpoint CSV-derived values. Self-test verifies the estimands differ on a known two-region example. Scientific source tables are not rewritten and no radiance corrections re-fitted.
