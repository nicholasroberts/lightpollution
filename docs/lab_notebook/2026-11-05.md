## Audit of Figure 1C versus Figure 1D radiance percentage change

Investigator noted Figure 1C visually rises from approximately 1.065 to 1.080 nW cm⁻² sr⁻¹ between 2014 and 2024 and asked why the histogram suggests an approximately +5% change; initial calculation was mis-stated as 13%. Correct arithmetic: (1.080 − 1.065)/1.065 = **1.408%**, not 13%. Figure 1D, not Figure 2D, is the histogram (`src/plot_publication_figure2_population_radiance.py::plot_2d` instead plots country-specific population–radiance scatter).

Code audit: `src/plot_publication_figure1_radiance.py::load_annual` builds Figure 1C from `data/processed/fixed_nuts2016_viirs_2014_2024.parquet` with `radiance_mean_corrected`, and a locked 2021 snow patch substituted at native pixels and aggregated to NUTS3. It calculates annual **European mean** by weighting each region's NUTS3 radiance by the same `valid_area_km2_2014` from the frozen 2014/2024 endpoints, then dividing by sum of areas. The previously uploaded Fig1C PDF explicitly displays **1,522 complete NUTS2016 regions**. The 2021-only snow adjustment cannot change 2014/2024 endpoint percentage.

Figure 1D `endpoint_summary` and `publication_hist` take the independent 1,522-region endpoint CSV `nuts3_radiance_spider_moth_cumulative_change_2014_2024.csv`, compute **regional percentages** `100*(R2024−R2014)/R2014` for every region with positive 2014 radiance, and calculate **equal-NUTS3 arithmetic mean and median**. Histogram uses a signed-log x scale and bins equal in transformed rather than untransformed percentage; its **mode is not the mean/median**. Crucially the percentage change of a weighted European mean corresponds to the regional percentages with **weights proportional to 2014 mapped area × 2014 starting radiance**, *plus the contribution of any initially exact-zero regions bright in 2024*. It need not equal the equal-region histogram centre; neither is an error in itself. Two figure endpoints originate from independent processed tables and are only checked in production with p99 absolute discrepancy tolerance 0.025; their exact agreement requires inspection of local exports.

## Figure 3A companion: radiance reduction for fixed +0.05 PRC

Investigator requested using the newly **linear-radiance Figure 3A** spider/moth fits to show how much **radiance decrease** is needed for exactly **+0.05 absolute polarization receptor contrast** from two starting points: 80 versus 5 nW cm⁻² sr⁻¹.

This is a deterministic inversion of the **independently locked exponential transfer** `P(R)=yf+(y0-yf)e^(-kR)` for each taxon, never a new fit, 2021 data correction, interpretation as a psychophysical detectability threshold, or measurement of realized radiance change. Solve `R_final = -log[(P(R_initial)+0.05-yf)/(y0-yf)]/k` then report `ΔR = R_initial-R_final`. Exact results:

- **Spider 80→17.60284**; drop **62.39716** nW (77.996%); initial PRC 0.145393, final PRC 0.195393.
- **Spider 5→2.69388**; drop **2.30612** nW (46.122%); initial PRC 0.330367, final PRC 0.380367.
- **Moth 80→31.17422**; drop **48.82578** nW (61.032%); initial PRC 0.102405, final PRC 0.152405.
- **Moth 5→3.48070**; drop **1.51930** nW (30.386%); initial PRC 0.497453, final PRC 0.547453.

For spider the absolute radiance reduction at 80 is about **27×** the reduction at 5; moth about **32×**. These are **absolute (+0.05) changes in dimensionless PRC**, not relative 5% increases. A physical reduction of night-time artificial lighting may have spectral and contextual effects outside this empirical scalar transfer.

## Exploratory DoP=0.23 equivalent threshold in annual spider PRC and regional area share

Investigator requested a visual threshold-centred remake of the existing 2014–2024 **annual absolute spider PRC level maps**, plus time series of the percentage area of Europe at/below an assumed DoP 0.23 navigation-performance level. This is exploratory receptor/behavioural context for a talk, NOT newly established spider sensitivity. Foster et al. (2019, *JEB*, DOI 10.1242/jeb.188532) documented appreciably **weaker but nonzero** nocturnal **dung beetle** orientation below DoP 0.23; this is not a validated Drassodes threshold.

Using orthogonal opposed receptor channels of polarization sensitivity `Sp=10` and **optimal e-vector alignment**, the idealised receptor log-contrast conversion is `PRC_threshold=2*atanh(0.23*(10-1)/(10+1)) = 0.3809031327477229`. Important: the threshold conversion is an assumption for context and does not modify the independently **locked empirical radiance-to-spider-PRC fit**. Lower PRC is not a proven loss of detectable polarization.

Implemented `src/plot_publication_spider_prc_dop023_threshold.py` and separate production document `docs/annual_nuts3_spider_prc_dop023_threshold.md` on **publication-figures**. The script reads the existing `historical_prc_2014_2024_spider_snow_corrected/prc_nuts3_annual_cumulative.csv` (1,522 NUTS2016 regions × all 11 years), with 2021 **already locked snow corrected**; no new pixels, sensor corrections, PRC fitting or regional recalculation. It renders a new 11-map 16:9 SVG/PDF/PNG atlas with the same annual radiance map geography and clean top labels, one pooled-and-fixed threshold-centred two-slope colourbar (warm red/orange below/at PRC .3809, yellow at threshold, cool green/teal above) and a marked threshold. The original unthresholded annual spider PRC maps and all original publication figures remain untouched.

**Area calculation definition matters:** For every year classify a region if its **existing native-pixel-derived NUTS3 mean PRC** is `<=0.380903`. Sum fixed **2014 PRC valid pixel coverage area** (`valid_area_km2_2014` per region) of whole classified NUTS3 regions, divide by fixed total among the **full 1,522-region study cohort**. This is the **fraction of fixed study area in below-threshold mean-PRC regions**, NOT an exact native-pixel below-threshold fraction. A secondary `continental_display_regions` series reports whole-region 2014 areas for NUTS3 polygons intersecting the annual map extent (which excludes far-flung territories and is not pixel-clipped geography). Year-specific valid-area sensitivity and counts are recorded separately. Data classification does not threshold mean radiance, and no region is imputed.
