## Refit completed in chat — provisional 16 matched PRC observations

A full unweighted fit of the **same R `SSasymp` function** was reconstructed in Python, `PRC(R) = yf + (y0-yf) * exp(-exp(log_alpha) * R)`, using the original 18-entry `sigg` vector (R script) and *approximate* plot-extracted radiances from the provided `Cali_Graph.pdf`. 16 of the 18 plotted points were provisionally matched to the 14 Table S1 coordinates; the two unlocated points were **not** assigned an EOG predictor.

For an exactly matched 16-point comparison, original plot radiances fitted `y0=0.458231, yf=0.144160, k=0.086388, R²=0.946384, RMSE=0.027910`. Replacing the radiance predictor with **our corrected EOG annual radiance in the actual field-observation year** fitted `y0=0.461175, yf=0.144845, k=0.106483, R²=0.949279, RMSE=0.027146`. The original 18 radiances alone fitted `y0=0.457217, yf=0.144451, k=0.084794, R²=0.945285`.

**Site-blocked leave-one-site-out RMSE** was `0.033863` (original 16) versus `0.032101` (corrected EOG 16). Sensitivity retaining **only 12 individually unambiguous plot-to-site assignments** gave original `R²=0.938785, RMSE=0.028320, k=0.079746` versus corrected EOG `R²=0.939345, RMSE=0.028190, k=0.102517`. These improvements are small, not a significance test. Particularly the calibration at the dark end and the proposed duplicate mappings remain provisional until the original filenames/`final.data` are recovered.

## Working PRC transfer function adopted — 19 October 2026

The investigator has expressly accepted, **for now**, the new exponential PRC relationship fitted to the project's **calibrated EOG VIIRS annual radiance in the field measurement year** as the *working function* for the next stages of historical PRC mapping. This supersedes the earlier intention to use the original website-based fit as the default. The original website fit remains a sensitivity comparator.

The provisional function is

```text
PRC(R) = 0.144845 + (0.461175 - 0.144845) * exp(-0.106483 * R)
       = 0.144845 + 0.316330 * exp(-0.106483 * R)
```

with corrected EOG radiance `R` in nW cm^-2 sr^-1. Fitted from the 16 provisionally site-matched observations drawn from the original 18-entry PRC vector, involving 14 Table S1 sites. Fitted goodness of fit `R²=0.949279`, `RMSE=0.027146`; site-blocked hold-out `RMSE=0.032101`. The fitted parameter triplet is rounded to six decimals for this frozen working definition; the fitting code retains full precision during fitting.

**Explicit provisional limits:** the R original measurement filenames are not yet recovered, four assignments are not unique, two original plotted observations have no Table S1 location, and a good in-sample fit does not validate behavioural visibility thresholds or cross-species/general-geography transport. The calibrated 2018/2019 response is a historically anchored empirical transfer relation, not a direct physical atmospheric scattering model. No new European PRC maps have been computed at this decision point.

The selected coefficients are also stored in the machine-readable repository file `config/prc_transfer.yaml` to avoid inadvertent use of the superseded website fit in later scripts. A formal fit/uncertainty sensitivity remains a later methodological step.

## First completed 2014–2024 native-pixel PRC reconstruction — outputs reviewed

The investigator supplied full results from `src/run_pipeline.py historical-prc`: `prc_europe_annual_cumulative.csv` (11 yearly rows), `prc_nuts3_annual_cumulative.csv` (16,742 region-years), `prc_nuts3_rankings_2014_2024.csv` (1,522 NUTS2016 region rows), and five PDFs (four 10-page annual/cumulative maps and a four-panel Europe trends figure). Every NUTS region was present all 11 years and `coverage_stable=True` in the current check. Area totals each year are ~5,857,860 km². **Coverage-stable does not establish strict matching of annual valid pixels.**

The provisional `PRC(R)=0.144845+0.316330*exp(-0.106483 R)` applied to the calibrated individual EOG pixels gives Europe (NUTS2016-covered area) an area-weighted mean PRC `0.442511` (2014) versus `0.441039` (2024): absolute `-0.001471`, relative `-0.332514%`. In 2020 the cumulative relative change was `-0.107178%`; an abrupt 2021 decline `-0.001790` (`-0.405027%` year/year) was followed by `+0.001209` (`+0.274573%`) in 2022. Of 37 countries, 35 showed a national mean decline from 2020 to 2021 and 34 rebounded from 2021 to 2022. **Do not interpret these swings as biological light-environment change without an independent 2021 snow/surface handling at pixel level.** The NUTS3-derived 2021 correction cannot be applied unchanged to native pixels.

Highest 2014→2024 **absolute mean-PRC gains**: `UKC23 Sunderland +0.067304 (+33.8349%)`, `UKK21 Bournemouth and Poole +0.059969 (+25.5253%)`, `UKG23 Stoke-on-Trent +0.056232 (+28.3447%)`, `UKC22 Tyneside +0.0538 (+22.76%)`, `UKG36 Dudley +0.0484 (+23.70%)`. Notable `NL333 Delft en Westland +0.024460 (+15.674%)`, ranked 78/1522 for absolute gain and 24/1522 for percent gain; `FR101 Paris +0.007232 (+4.83%)`.

Highest **absolute mean-PRC losses**: `TR100 Istanbul -0.038554 (-10.6258%)`, `TR631 Hatay -0.0359 (-8.50%)`, `RO322 Ilfov -0.031853 (-8.18%)`, `TR902 Ordu -0.0299 (-6.77%)`, `TR421 Kocaeli -0.0267 (-6.68%)`. At country area-weighted scale, BE `+5.50%`, FR `+1.99%`, UK `+0.98%`; TR `-2.36%`, EL `-1.28%`, RO `-1.15%`.

**Crucial audit before a manuscript:** annual source filenames are **VNL V2.1 2014–2021**, a **V2.2 NPP-J01-labelled input in 2022**, and differently named `v2` products for 2023–2024. Not assume these processing/version transitions are radiometrically seamless. Belgium's national mean PRC was ~0.3745 in 2020, ~0.3820 in 2022 and ~0.3958 in 2023, so a large part of its apparent decade gain occurs near the source-product transition. Conversely, many largest UK gains (e.g. Sunderland, Bournemouth and Poole, Stoke-on-Trent) evolve in several earlier years and deserve separate instrument/version-robust validation. Country-specific sign uniformity (all Turkish regions losing PRC, all Belgian regions gaining) is diagnostic evidence requiring physical and processing verification, **not causal attribution**.

The current NUTS2016 atlas is an **aggregation/presentation layer** over native-pixel PRC calculation; it does not yet provide direct mapped native-pixel annual changes unless the optional GeoTIFF stage is run. Next priority: 2021 pixel-level snow/coverage diagnosis; 2022–2024 cross-product/version consistency especially Belgium and northern/western Europe; stable common-pixel mask; sensitivity to transfer-function uncertainty; compare 2014–2020 and 2022–2024 segments, then publish regional rankings with quality flags.
