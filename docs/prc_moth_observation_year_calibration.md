# Moth DRA polarization-receptor contrast: companion site-level calibration (20 October 2026)

## Objective and R-code provenance

Reproduce the moth **DRAc** contrast-versus-radiance fit in the user's original R workflow alongside, **but independently from**, the already locked spider PRC calibration.

Original R selections supplied by investigator:

- \`filenames <- list.files(path)\`
- \`filenames <- filenames[-c(1,5)]\` for moth, compared with \`filenames[-1]\` for spider.
- \`DRAc <- c(0.407, 0.603, 0.692, 0.239, 0.159, 0.093, 0.448, 0.206, 0.378, 0.539, 0.620, 0.629, 0.142, 0.211, 0.071, 0.109, 0.216)\`.
- \`ppr=laply(filenames, ... mean(value), sd(value))\` from original monthly VIIRS CSV.
- \`nls(signal ~ SSasymp(rad, yf, y0, log_alpha), data=...)\`, same saturating exponential used for spider.

The original \`FINALVIIRSdata.zip\` contains 19 source CSV files; alphabetical \`list.files()\` orders \`Bristol.csv\` first and \`Gavarnie.csv\` fifth, so the moth 17 have **Bristol and Gavarnie excluded**. This is not a low-radiance exclusion; it is the exact original R moth source selection. Original site filename order and moth DRAc values preserved in \`data/reference/prc_moth_17_observations_from_original_r.csv\`; the script **asserts both filename order and all 17 values** before joining EOG site records.

Same confirmed original site coordinates and observation nights as the spider analysis; observation-year EOG 2018 for Cottage and Skillinge (2 sites); 2019 for remaining 15 (including Slabhouse). The EOG 2017+ \`-0.235\` correction was applied ONCE in the existing confirmed site extracts; do not apply it again.

## Moth fits on recovered data

Canonical R nonlinear model in Python: \`moth_PRC(R) = yf + (y0 - yf) exp(-k R)\`, \`k=exp(log_alpha)\`. Same multi-start least-squares solver family as the original Python spider refit.

| Predictor/cohort | n | yf | y0 | k | R² | Fit RMSE | Satellite-pixel CV RMSE |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Historical monthly VIIRS mean, all original moth observations | 17 | .074426 | .664936 | .054893 | .958425 | .042283 | .052053 |
| Corrected field-year annual EOG, all moth observations | 17 | .094529 | .666772 | .072699 | .950968 | .045918 | .056158 |
| Historical monthly VIIRS mean, excluding Slabhouse | 16 | .079785 | .676024 | .057327 | .954790 | .042761 | .054887 |
| **Corrected field-year annual EOG, excluding Slabhouse (preferred)** | **16** | **.101283** | **.687095** | **.078231** | **.949448** | **.045217** | **.058352** |

The original Slabhouse **moth PRC=0.620 remains an authentic biological observation** in the source and all-17 comparison, but the official annual EOG 2018/2019 raw VIIRS value at that location is exactly zero (despite original monthly radiance 0.498 nW cm^-2 sr^-1). The preferred model excludes this **satellite observation** for the same specific quality reason accepted in the spider analysis, not merely for its effect on R².

**Preferred pending-review moth function**:

\`PRC_moth(R) = 0.101283345240 + 0.585811529445 * exp(-0.078231203694 * R)\`.

This is appreciably different from locked spider:
\`PRC_spider(R) = 0.145315902063 + 0.310812454144 * exp(-0.103711560708 * R)\`.

Moth has 16 site PRCs but **15 independent annual VIIRS measurement-year pixel coordinates**: Oxford Open Square and St Anne's College share a nominal 2019 VIIRS pixel; pixel-block CV therefore withholds both together. Moth pixel-blocked RMSE 0.05835, which is greater in absolute PRC units than spider's 0.03393, but response ranges differ—don't infer relative biological model superiority from RMSE alone. Source nighttime, annual compositing, geolocation, original filename duplicate, LED spectral and atmospheric uncertainty must be reported.

The two curves are descriptive **modelled polarization photoreceptor contrast**, not demonstrated behavioural visibility thresholds. Do not insert spectrally inferred LED-corrected broadband radiance into a DNB-trained transfer without defining the new predictor and validating its physiological meaning.
