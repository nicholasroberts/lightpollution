## Source and estimands

All **1,273** reconstructed complete NUTS2016 regions, 2014→2020. Predictor: \`Jan1 population / fixed land area\`, reported as **absolute Δdensity people/km²**, not percentage growth. Outcomes: (1) endpoint change in corrected VIIRS NUTS3 mean radiance, (2) locked spider native-pixel-derived NUTS3 mean PRC change, (3) locked moth equivalent. 2021 snow correction is not part of either endpoint. This dataset is the investigator-selected demographic source for analysis, but its previously reported independent boundary/status QA caveats are unchanged. No biological fit or radiance calibration was altered.

## NL333, Delft en Westland: geographical and temporal checks

NL333 is present in the **40** matched Dutch regions; its fixed land area is **154 km²**, **0.450%** of the summed matched Dutch land area (34,188 km²). From the uploaded Dutch region-level CSV:

| Measure | NL333 2014 | NL333 2019 | NL333 2020 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Corrected VIIRS regional mean (nW cm−2 sr−1) | 275.8668 | 264.8900 | 225.0218 |
| Jan1 population density change from 2014 (people/km²) | 0 | +61.1299 | +75.1169 |
| Spider ΔPRC from 2014 | 0 | −.002210 | +.001565 |
| Moth ΔPRC from 2014 | 0 | −.005378 | +.004036 |

Thus **2014→2020** NL333 ΔR=**−50.845 nW cm−2 sr−1** (−18.43% of baseline), the **largest absolute radiance change of all 40 Dutch NUTS3** in this interval. Yet it is only **25th of 40** by absolute spider ΔPRC and **19th of 40** by absolute moth ΔPRC. This supports a large regional-radiance/biological-contrast magnitude mismatch, consistent with extreme baseline radiance and spatially nonlinear pixel transformations, not proof of a particular greenhouse-lighting policy.

**Endpoint warning:** about **78.4%** of NL333's six-year decrease in regional mean radiance occurs **2019→2020**: −39.868 of −50.845 nW. Spider and moth cumulative ΔPRC **switch signs** across the two endpoints (negative at 2019, positive at 2020), despite negative regional mean ΔR in both. Do NOT collapse that result into a single stable Dutch LED/switch-off mechanism, and do not substitute its earlier 2014→2024 −179.5 nW result.

| Corrected VIIRS ΔR | −.338 | −.290 |
| Spider ΔPRC | +.182 | +.137 |

The NL population/radiance anticorrelation is **not created solely by NL333**. Radiance **Pearson** changes from −.469 to −.709 when it is removed, showing much greater outlier/leverage sensitivity than the rank correlation. Dutch radiance/PRC relationships should not be interpreted from the extreme endpoint value alone.

Using the fixed land-area weights as a transparent regional aggregation sensitivity, the 2014–2020 matched Dutch **area-weighted** mean changes are Δradiance **−.99292 nW**, Δspider PRC **−.001053**, Δmoth PRC **−.001440**, Δdensity **+16.681 people/km²**. Thus net Dutch modelled PRC change is negative even though area-weighted mean VIIRS radiance decreased. This is possible because the PRC is transformed at native pixels before region averaging, and distinct regions can have opposing changes. The area-weighted PRC estimate is a **land-area proxy**, not an exact common-valid-pixel national aggregate.

NL333 supplies **−.22903 nW** to that national area-weighted mean Δradiance (23.1% of the **net** −.99292) while containing just 0.450% of the matched Dutch land area. **This fraction is sensitive to cancellation** among positive and negative contributions and must not be called a share of total dimming or all emitted light. Its contribution to national mean PRC is only +.00000705 spider and +.00001818 moth, with opposite sign to the Dutch overall decline.

### Local polygon neighbours

The study uses real EPSG:3035 polygon boundary-to-boundary distances, not straight lines between region names or municipal locations. NL333 has only **2** directly touching Dutch NUTS3 neighbours: **NL332 and NL33C**; n=3 including NL333 is too small for a defensible local correlation. For the 2014→2020 changes:

| Set | n | Regions with Δpopulation density >0 | ΔR <0 | Δspider PRC >0 | Δmoth PRC >0 | rho(Δdensity, ΔR) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| NL333 and all regions within 25 km boundary distance | 8 | 8 | 7 | 4 | 5 | −.548 |
| NL333 and all regions within 50 km boundary distance | 15 | 15 | 10 | 7 | 8 | −.507 |
| NL333 excluded, other regions within 25 km | 7 | 7 | 6 | 3 | 4 | −.643 |

The principal **within-25-km** neighbours are: NL332, NL33C (touching), NL337, NL33B, NL33A, NL329 and NL342. All eight regions **gained population density**, whereas **seven decreased VIIRS radiance**; their PRC signs differ. In particular, neighbouring **NL332** gained +206.50 people/km² and lost −18.24 nW while **spider PRC increased +.00839**; **NL33C** gained +30.39 people/km² and lost −12.08 nW but **spider PRC fell −.00041** (moth +.00128). Same-direction regional *mean radiance* change therefore does not guarantee matching signs/magnitudes in spatially averaged nonlinear receptor models.

The same Dutch file permits an unplanned **2014→2019 local temporal sensitivity** without changing the main 2014→2020 analysis:
- NL population–radiance Spearman **−.465** (2014→2019; n40), versus −.338 in 2014→2020;
- NL population–spider PRC Spearman **+.117** in 2014→2019 versus +.182 in 2014→2020;
- Within 25 km of NL333, 8/8 regions grew population and 6/8 reduced radiance by 2019 (versus 7/8 in 2020).

This provides some directional robustness to the Dutch population/radiance decoupling but **not** stability of NL333's biological PRC sign, which reversed between 2019 and 2020.

## Scientific interpretation and next source QA

**What the data show:** Demographic growth does not predict the same radiance/PRC change across all countries or Dutch regional surroundings. Some regions (TR, DE) show the expected negative population–PRC rank pattern, others opposite (FR, NL, EL), and national cross-country ranks are modest and inclusion-sensitive. NL333 is an extreme *radiance* endpoint outlier but not a proportionate *biological PRC* endpoint outlier.

**What they do not show:** absence of causal demographic contribution; source-specific Dutch horticultural/greenhouse changes; LED spectra or nighttime operations at NL333; spatially independent confidence intervals; direct organism outcomes. PRC is a deterministic biological mapping based on the VIIRS light field, not a separate environmental observation.
